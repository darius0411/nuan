隐私政策 · Privacy Policy

最后更新 Last updated: May 2025

核心承诺：暖暖不出售、不共享你的个人数据。你的聊天记录、情绪日记、个人档案默认只存在你自己的设备上。

1. 我们收集什么信息

  • 你主动提供的内容——与暖暖的对话、情绪打卡、日记文字、昵称与简介
  • 自动生成的数据——由对话分析出的性格标签与记忆片段（「暖暖眼中的我」功能）

我们不收集：真实姓名、电话、身份证、精确位置、通讯录、相机或麦克风数据。

2. 数据存储在哪里

  • 免费版：所有数据仅存储在你设备的浏览器本地存储（localStorage）中，不上传至任何服务器。
  • AI 对话：你发送的消息会传输至暖暖 AI 后端（托管于 Render.com）以生成回复，处理后不长期保留。
  • 守候版：启用云端同步后，数据会加密存储于 Firebase（Google）。

3. 数据如何使用

  • 生成 AI 回复（对话内容发送后端处理后即丢弃）
  • 在你的设备上本地生成性格分析与记忆摘要
  • 改善应用体验（不涉及个人身份识别）

我们不会将你的数据用于广告投放或出售给第三方。

4. 第三方服务

  • Anthropic Claude API：驱动 AI 对话。适用 Anthropic 隐私政策
  • Render.com：AI 后端托管
  • GitHub Pages：应用托管
  • Firebase / Google（守候版）：云端同步，适用 Google 隐私政策

5. 你对数据的控制权

  • 删除：设置页面「清除所有数据」可删除全部本地数据
  • 查看：所有本地数据可通过浏览器开发者工具查看
  • 导出：功能规划中

6. 儿童隐私

暖暖面向 13 岁及以上用户。我们不会故意收集 13 岁以下儿童的个人信息。如有此情况请联系我们删除。

Our commitment: Nuan does not sell or share your personal data. Your chats, mood logs, and profile are stored locally on your device by default.

1. What We Collect

  • Content you provide — conversations with Nuan, mood check-ins, journal entries, nickname and bio
  • Derived data — personality tags and memory snippets generated from conversations ("How Lumi Sees Me" feature)

We do not collect: real name, phone number, ID, precise location, contacts, camera or microphone data.

2. Where Data Is Stored

  • Free version: All data is stored only in your device's browser localStorage. Nothing is uploaded to a server.
  • AI conversations: Messages are sent to Nuan's AI backend (hosted on Render.com) to generate responses. They are not retained after processing.
  • Premium: When cloud sync is enabled, data is encrypted and stored on Firebase (Google).

3. How Data Is Used

  • Generating AI responses (conversation content is discarded after processing)
  • Locally generating personality analysis and memory summaries on your device
  • Improving the app experience (no personal identification)

We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Anthropic Claude API: Powers AI chat. Subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy
  • Render.com: AI backend hosting
  • GitHub Pages: App hosting
  • Firebase / Google (Premium): Cloud sync. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy

5. Your Controls

  • Delete: "Clear All Data" in Settings removes all local data
  • View: All local data can be inspected via your browser's developer tools
  • Export: Planned feature

6. Children's Privacy

Nuan is for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Please contact us if this has occurred.
